01 / READ FIRST

Art direction and replacement rules

Production art must be human-created. These exports and screenshots are documentary references only; they are not replacement assets. Existing placeholder sheets remain until each human-made replacement has been checked in game. Audio is outside this visual handoff.

Visual language

  • Portraits and icons: vector artwork.
  • World art: pixel art. Preserve nearest-neighbor sampling and the 2× prototype render scale; one source-art pixel occupies two world units.
  • Hero: a female angel in a purple cloak, riding a black ox, with light spear/bow. Existing sheets face right and the game mirrors them.
  • Do not treat procedural boss patterns, names, layout, balance or enemy tints as approved final character designs. They describe current test visuals only.

Replacement workflow

  1. Use the source canvas, tags, facing and alignment shown below.
  2. Keep editable human-made sources with exports and update art/CREDITS.md with creator, rights and status.
  3. Give each shared/tinted role its own readable silhouette or directed treatment; tint alone is not a final roster.
  4. Check silhouettes, grips, warnings, hit areas and text-free runtime composition at game scale before replacing the old reference.

Not exhaustively pictured — read before drawing

  • Procedural permutations: one representative render is supplied for lava layouts, arena decoration, wall orientation, obstacle crack paths and debris. Every random seed and every frame of debris motion is not a separate deliverable.
  • Time-based shader motion and transitions: stills show representative glow, haze, pulse, shimmer, descent and healed states. They cannot verify animation cadence, bloom over time or final feel; check those in Godot.
  • Runtime-generated timing/cache atlases: excluded as duplicates. The gallery covers original Aseprite source frames and stored tags instead.
  • Mirror selector: has no unique draw call of its own. Its actual spear-thrust and bow-shot outcomes are pictured separately; it is not falsely listed as uncovered art.
  • Editor-only gizmos and debug collision shapes: not production-facing art and not included.
  • Original Aseprite sources: linked to their unchanged workspace files for replacement. The original PNG/SVG images requested for portability are included inside this folder.

Recommended first replacement check: the female purple-cloaked angel on the black ox, one husk, the light spear, and corrupted floor in the opening encounter. Judge silhouette, grip, contrast and 2× world scale before drawing the rest.
