# Pafta sprite strip credits

The PNG files in this folder are unmodified copies of Aseprite horizontal-strip exports prepared for the EIGHT jury presentation. They were exported at 4× from the owner-approved alpha `.aseprite` sources in `eight/assets/art/placeholder/` by `export_sprites.ps1`; no colour, filter, or artwork changes were made for this poster.

The underlying sprites are temporary code-generated placeholder art, not hand-made production art. Their source is the EIGHT owner's 21-colour palette and `tools/placeholder_art.lua`. Source and redistribution notes for the underlying assets are recorded in `eight/assets/art/placeholder/CREDITS.md`; EIGHT project assets may be reused within this project presentation under those existing terms.
